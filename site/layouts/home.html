$ brew install somad

How it works

  1. TUI & CLI

    soma opens the interface; soma play, soma next, soma status and friends work without it.

  2. Daemon

    Starts on demand, streams and decodes the audio, tracks titles, shows the tray icon, and keeps playing after every client has gone.

  3. SomaFM

    Plays each channel's best stream directly, AAC on macOS and MP3 on Linux, and reconnects on its own when the network hiccups.

Frontends and daemon talk over a local socket, or over TCP with TLS and a pre-shared key when the speakers live on another machine. Remote control details.
